Privacy policy

The game stays on your device

Mariáš / Marias does not operate a game server, require an account, display ads or include developer analytics or tracking. The game does not automatically send your hand, progress, settings or statistics to us.

Local information

Saved hands, preferences, lesson completion and game-point statistics are stored locally. Removing the app may remove this information. Any device backup is controlled by your operating system, not a cloud service operated by us.

Purchases and platform services

Your app-store provider processes the paid download and may provide its own account, payment, backup and aggregate store-reporting services under its privacy policy. The game does not request your payment details and has no in-app purchase backend.

Audio and permissions

Narration and sound effects are bundled files. Some audio was created with ElevenLabs during development. The installed game does not contact ElevenLabs or record your microphone. Optional haptic feedback uses the device vibration API.

Contacting support

If you email us, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to answer your request. We do not automatically attach game data or diagnostics. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; retention may be necessary to handle the request or applicable obligations.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy. The pages use locally hosted fonts and do not add advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or cookies.

Publisher and contact

Published by Malkiel Shaul / AppTuneLabs. For privacy questions or support: apptuneslabs@gmail.com. This policy describes the offline paid game; changes to these practices will be reflected here.

Need a hand?

Email apptuneslabs@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Ochrana soukromí

Hra zůstává v zařízení

Stažení za platbu zpracovává provozovatel obchodu. Jeho služby účtu, platby, zálohování a souhrnných přehledů se řídí jeho zásadami soukromí. Hra nevyžaduje platební údaje a nemá systém nákupů v aplikaci.
