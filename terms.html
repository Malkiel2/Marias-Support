The game

Mariáš is a recreational game with computer opponents and game points. It offers no money betting, cash prizes or redeemable currency. Game rules and supported variants are described in the rulebook. Coach suggestions use limited public information and are not guaranteed outcomes.

Optional Premium

In version 1.0, monthly and yearly subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime purchase unlock the same Premium features. The store displays the actual local price and any trial eligibility before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple or Google subscription settings. Cancelling renewal normally leaves access until the paid period ends. Lifetime has one charge and no subscription renewal.

Restore, refunds and saved data

Restore purchases uses the same Apple or Google account in that store. Purchases are not transferred between stores. Refund requests and billing are handled by the store. Game progress and hand history are stored on the device; a purchase restore restores access, not your local saved games. Keep your device backups if you want to preserve local data.

Volitelné Premium

Ve verzi 1.0 měsíční a roční předplatné i jednorázový nákup navždy odemykají stejné funkce Premium. Skutečnou místní cenu a způsobilost ke zkušební době ukazuje obchod před potvrzením. Předplatné se automaticky obnovuje do zrušení v nastavení Apple nebo Google. Po zrušení obnovování zpravidla zůstává přístup do konce zaplaceného období. Nákup navždy má jednu platbu a neobnovuje se.
